## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Material is selected locally before generation
The system SHALL decide crawl versus upload before generation. A document is a crawl when any source file has a `source_url`; otherwise it is an upload. Crawl material SHALL be the downloaded source pages in file order. Upload material SHALL be the sections returned by the sections endpoint. Selection MUST NOT call a model. When the page count or section count is less than or equal to 20, the system MUST feed that whole batch. When the count is greater than 20, simple questions MUST use a systematic sample of 5 pages or 5 sections, always including the first and the last. Multi-hop material MUST be selected separately and MUST NOT exceed 20 distinct pages or sections after deduplication. Vague, adversarial, and comprehensive questions MUST use the multi-hop batch when the count is greater than 20, and the full batch when the count is less than or equal to 20. MMR MUST NOT be used.

#### Scenario: Small crawl feeds every page
- **WHEN** a crawl document has 20 or fewer source pages
- **THEN** simple, multi-hop, and custom questions are all generated from those pages

#### Scenario: Large crawl samples five pages for simple questions
- **WHEN** a crawl document has more than 20 source pages
- **THEN** simple questions are generated only from 5 systematically sampled pages, including the first page and the last page

#### Scenario: Large upload uses multi-hop sections for custom questions
- **WHEN** an uploaded document splits into more than 20 sections
- **THEN** vague, adversarial, and comprehensive questions are generated from the multi-hop sections and not from the simple-question sample

### Requirement: Crawl multi-hop pairs one in-corpus link per start
For a crawl larger than 20 pages, the system SHALL take one start per multi-hop question by systematic sampling over crawl order. A URL that appears on more than half of those start pages MUST be dropped as navigation. Each start MUST keep at most one remaining link that points at another page in the same document. When a start has no such link, the system MUST walk forward to the next page and MUST keep the following start on its original interval. The paired pages, after deduplication, MUST NOT exceed 20. Uploaded documents MUST pair adjacent sections that share the same non-empty parent title instead of page links.

#### Scenario: Repeated link is dropped as navigation
- **WHEN** more than half of the sampled start pages link to the same in-corpus URL
- **THEN** that URL is not used as a multi-hop pair target

#### Scenario: Missing link walks forward without shifting the next start
- **WHEN** a sampled start page has no qualified in-corpus link and a later page before the next planned start does
- **THEN** the pair uses that later page and the next planned start index stays on the original interval

#### Scenario: Upload pairs sibling sections
- **WHEN** two neighboring sections share the same non-empty parent title
- **THEN** they can form one multi-hop pair

### Requirement: RAGAS calls follow the material batches
The system SHALL generate simple and multi-hop questions with RAGAS `generate_with_langchain_docs`. When the batch is greater than 20, the system MUST call RAGAS twice: the simple call MUST include only `SingleHopSpecificQuerySynthesizer`, and the multi-hop call MUST include only `MultiHopSpecificQuerySynthesizer` and `MultiHopAbstractQuerySynthesizer`. When the multi-hop material has no relationships, that call MUST yield zero questions and MUST NOT add the single-hop synthesizer. When the batch is less than or equal to 20, the system MUST call RAGAS once with all three synthesizers and split the simple-plus-multi-hop count across the synthesizers that can run. Comprehensive questions MUST NOT be generated by RAGAS.

#### Scenario: Large document uses two RAGAS calls
- **WHEN** generation runs for a document with more than 20 pages or sections
- **THEN** simple questions and multi-hop questions are produced by separate RAGAS calls on their own materials

#### Scenario: Multi-hop without relationships stays empty
- **WHEN** the multi-hop material cannot form a RAGAS relationship
- **THEN** the multi-hop batch contributes zero questions and simple-question generation is not repeated to fill them

### Requirement: Question counts follow 4:2:2:1:1
The system SHALL split `question_count` into simple, multi-hop, comprehensive, vague, and adversarial using weights 4, 2, 2, 1, and 1. The remainder after dividing by 10 MUST be added to the simple count. For 50 questions the counts MUST be 20, 10, 10, 5, and 5. The number of multi-hop pairs MUST equal the multi-hop question count, subject to the 20-page or 20-section cap.

#### Scenario: Fifty questions use the fixed mix
- **WHEN** the dataset requests 50 questions
- **THEN** the plan assigns 20 simple, 10 multi-hop, 10 comprehensive, 5 vague, and 5 adversarial questions

#### Scenario: Remainder goes to simple questions
- **WHEN** the requested count is not divisible by 10
- **THEN** the leftover questions are added to the simple count and the five counts still sum to the requested total

### Requirement: Custom questions cover vague, adversarial, and comprehensive
The system SHALL write vague, adversarial, and comprehensive questions with the eval dataset chat model from the custom material batch. A vague question MUST be an unclear wording of something the material can answer. An adversarial question MUST ask for something the material does not contain, and its stored ground truth MUST be `不知道`. A comprehensive question MUST ask for a summary or synthesis of that batch. Each saved item MUST keep the question, ground truth, and reference excerpts.

#### Scenario: Adversarial ground truth is unknown
- **WHEN** an adversarial question is saved
- **THEN** its ground truth is `不知道`

#### Scenario: Comprehensive question uses the custom batch
- **WHEN** comprehensive questions are generated for a document larger than 20 pages or sections
- **THEN** their source text is the multi-hop batch
